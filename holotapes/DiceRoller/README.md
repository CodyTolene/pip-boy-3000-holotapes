# Dice Roller

By [@CodyTolene](https://github.com/CodyTolene).

### Description & Options

A simple D2 through D100 dice roller with modifiers, targets and critical
ranges.

- Available die: d2, d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100.
- Dice count: 1–10 dice of the selected type.
- Modifier: -99 to +99, added once to the sum of all dice.
- Target total: OFF or 1–1099. A modified total at or above the target succeeds.
- Critical face: OFF or 1 through the die's maximum face.
